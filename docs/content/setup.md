# First setup

Do this once after [install](install.md). You need about ten minutes and the
people who will use the app.

## 1. Sign in and change the admin password

The first account is **admin** / **admin**. Click the username in the header
to open the **User Control Panel**, then **Change password**. Use at least
eight characters.

Keep this account for household administration. You can add more parent
logins later.

## 2. Create the family

On **Home**, enter a family name (for example “The Smith Family”) and create
it. The first parent to do this becomes the household owner. One StarApp
install is one family.

## 3. Add people

Open **Control Panel → People** (or **Add person** on Home).

For each person:

1. Enter their **display name** (what the family sees).
2. Turn **login** on only if they will sign in themselves — typically each
   child who should see their own stars.
3. If login is on, set a username and a password of at least eight
   characters. Share those credentials with them in person.

A star colour is assigned automatically. You can upload an avatar later from
their person page.

People without a login still appear on the family home and star chart. A
parent awards stars and redeems rewards on their behalf.

## 4. Add rewards

Open **Rewards** in the header. Create a few privileges your family actually
uses, for example:

| Reward | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Extra screen time | 1 star |
| Choose dinner | 5 stars |
| Stay up 30 minutes late | 5 stars |

Each reward can require **parent approval** before stars are spent. That is
the default for new rewards unless you change it in
[Settings](configuration.md).

## 5. Add a star chart and chores

Open **Star Charts** to create a weekly chart, then **Control Panel →
Chores** to add chores:

- Title (for example “Do your homework”)
- Stars earned when completed
- Weekdays the chore is due
- Which people it applies to
- Which star chart it belongs to

The chart is a Monday–Sunday grid. Completing a scheduled cell awards those
stars.

## 6. Hand a child their login

Give the child the username and password you created. They open the same URL
as you, sign in, and land on **their** home — own balance, recent awards,
and rewards they can redeem. They do not see siblings’ balances or the
Control Panel.

## Checklist

- [ ] Admin password changed
- [ ] Family named
- [ ] People added (with login where needed)
- [ ] A few rewards exist
- [ ] At least one chore is on a star chart
- [ ] Children can sign in on a tablet or phone

[Using StarApp](using.md) covers the daily loop.
